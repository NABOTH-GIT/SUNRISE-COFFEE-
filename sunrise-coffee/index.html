Our story

Sunrise Coffee started with one espresso machine and a simple idea: a neighbourhood café that is open for the early risers, the night-shift workers and everyone in between.

We roast in small batches, bake every morning and buy directly from farms we trust. Whether you stop for a quick espresso or settle in for the afternoon, you are always welcome here.
